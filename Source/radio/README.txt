Drop your own music here.

Any .mp3 or .pda file in this folder becomes a station on the cab radio, named after the file.
(.wav and .aiff files are converted to .pda when the game is built.)

You can also add files without rebuilding: copy .mp3 or .pda files into the game's
Data/<bundleID>/radio/ folder (com.playdategame.excavator) on the Playdate or in the simulator.

Only use audio you have the right to use.
